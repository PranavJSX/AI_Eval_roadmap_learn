Company Remote Work Policy:
Employees are permitted to work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, during which employees must be available on communication channels. Equipment provided by the company must remain secure at home offices.

Company Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy:
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year. PTO requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance for vacations exceeding three consecutive days. Unused PTO does not roll over into the next calendar year and will be paid out conditionally upon departure.

Expense Reimbursement Guidelines:
Meals during approved business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day with itemized receipts. Flight bookings must be made through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days prior to departure to qualify for full reimbursement. Hotel accommodations are capped at $200 per night.
